\section*{Model}

The main claim is that inertia is not fixed. In a stable environment, inertia can grow because people stop looking broadly across the display. We therefore model utility as a function of the attributes, an inertia term, and an interaction between inertia and attention:

\begin{equation}
U_{n,t,j} =
V(X_{n,t,j}, \beta_{n,t})
+ \beta_{\text{Inertia},0} I_{n,t,j}
+ \beta_{\text{Att}} \text{LatAttention}_{n,t} I_{n,t,j}
+ \epsilon_{n,t,j}.
\end{equation}

Here, $I_{n,t,j}$ indexes whether alternative $j$ is supported by the learned layout or by the previous choice, and $\text{LatAttention}_{n,t}$ is the participant's attention on trial $t$. The effective inertia parameter is therefore trial-specific:

\begin{equation}
\beta_{\text{Inertia},n,t} =
\beta_{\text{Inertia},0}
+ \beta_{\text{Inertia-Attention},n}
\text{LatAttention}_{n,t}.
\end{equation}

Attention is tied to eye-tracking through a measurement equation based on gaze entropy:

\begin{equation}
\text{Entropy}_{n,t} =
f(\text{LatAttention}_{n,t}) + \varepsilon_{n,t}.
\end{equation}

This structural approach matters because choices alone cannot distinguish a deliberate repeated choice from a habit caused by the stable layout. If attention is omitted, the model pushes the missing term into the error:

\begin{equation}
\hat{\epsilon}_{n,t,j} =
\beta_{\text{Att}} \text{LatAttention}_{n,t} I_{n,t,j}
+ \epsilon_{n,t,j}.
\end{equation}

Because reliance on the learned layout is related to lower attention, the omitted term is correlated with inertia:

\begin{equation}
\operatorname{Cov}(I_{n,t,j}, \hat{\epsilon}_{n,t,j}) \neq 0.
\end{equation}

Standard approaches that omit this latent attention interaction suffer from observational equivalence—they cannot distinguish a blind habit from a calculated choice. By resolving this endogeneity using eye-tracking as a structural measurement equation, our model can accurately isolate true preference parameters. Furthermore, attribute coefficients and willingness-to-pay are allowed to change dynamically over trials and shift after the layout disruption, capturing the true evolution of valuation.
